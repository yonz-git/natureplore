Natureplore design system

Delicate frosted glass over a darkened forest photograph, white type in light weights, one olive lime for the thing to do next, and room around every group.

This page is the human-readable mirror of docs/design.md, which is the source of truth. Every value here comes from the tokens in :root. Numbers in labels are pixels at the default 16px root; the tokens themselves are in rem.

Colors

One ground, two strengths of glass, white type, and one lime used in six places only; a lime phrase closes the page title and no other heading. The group colours tint icons, dots and the proportion bar, always beside a name.

Motion

Motion shows what changed and nothing else: one strong ease-out, cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1), on transform and opacity only.

control or chip
200 to 250ms
dropdown or expanding list
250ms
screen or sheet
under 400ms, surface then content
hover
colour or wash, 200ms
reduced motion
a fade instead of movement, 150ms

Never animate an ancestor of glass; animate each layer. The welcome's scroll opening is the one choreographed sequence.

Do's and don'ts

  • Keep one lime action per screen and let everything else be glass, inner glass or text.
  • Set radius by depth, 28 then 18 then 12, and every other pill at half its height.
  • Keep the dark filter on every photograph and check white text on its brightest part.
  • Pin columns to both sides so left and right margins match.
  • Give a spot row's facts their own lines, with the Latin name in italic.
  • Keep targets at 44 or more, and 52 to 54 for the main controls.
  • Check caption and secondary text at white 78% on the brightest part of each photograph; if it falls under 4.5:1, darken the filter, never thicken the type.
  • Don't use lime anywhere outside its six places, give more than one heading a lime phrase, or put group colours behind text.
  • Don't stack a blurred surface on another blurred surface (the one exception is the save orb on a photograph), or thicken the rim past a glint.
  • Don't let a bar's border eat its padding: the height includes padding on both sides plus the 0.5px border.
  • Don't round images into circles, or set text heavier than 500 or in capitals.
  • Don't join facts with middle dots. Use commas, or stack them when a row carries more than two.
  • Don't let a row in a flex column shrink to fit; grow the board instead.
